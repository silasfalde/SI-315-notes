\chapter{Balance}

\section{Balance Theory}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Balance theory: suggests that triads tend to evolve toward a balanced state, where the product of the signs of the edges is positive
    \item Balanced triads: 3 positive edges or 1 positive edge
    \item Unbalanced triads: one negative edge and two positive edges or all negative edges
    \item Balanced network: all triads are balanced
\end{itemize}

\section{Weak Balance Theorem}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Weak balance theorem: in a signed network, if all triads are weakly balanced (no triad has exactly two negative edges), the network can be partitioned into two mutually antagonistic groups with positive ties within each group and negative ties between groups
    \item Weakly balanced triads: triads that have 3 positive edges, 1 positive edge, or 0 positive edges
    \item Weakly balanced network: all triads in the network are weakly balanced
\end{itemize}
